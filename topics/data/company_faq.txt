Acme Gadgets - Customer FAQ (sample data for the CrewAI examples)

Returns: Items can be returned within 45 days of delivery if unused and in the
original packaging. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within
7 business days after the item reaches our warehouse.

Warranty: All Acme devices include a 2-year limited warranty covering
manufacturing defects. Water damage and accidental drops are not covered.

Shipping: Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days. Express shipping takes
1-2 business days and costs 12 USD. Orders above 75 USD ship free (standard).

Support hours: Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 18:00 IST. Email support@acme.example.
Phone support is available only for customers with an active premium plan.

Premium plan: 9 USD per month. Includes priority support, free express
shipping, and an extended 3-year warranty on new purchases.
